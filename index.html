How to play: Use your arrow keys to move the tiles. When two tiles with the same symbol touch, they get promoted!

A stormtrooper at 2 is where this board begins, and the Death Star at 2048 is where it ends. In between stand a super trooper, Darth Vader, a TIE fighter, an x-wing, the Millennium Falcon, an AT-AT, C-3PO, R2-D2 and Yoda: eleven tiles, one per value, each with its own picture instead of a number. Arrow keys or a swipe move all of them together, and two identical pictures fuse into the next one up, scoring as they go. Keep spare squares and think a couple of moves ahead: a full board with no matching pair ends the run. Assemble the Death Star to clear the galaxy.
